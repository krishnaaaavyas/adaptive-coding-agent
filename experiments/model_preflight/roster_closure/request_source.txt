==================================================
9. MINIMUM VS EXTENDED VERIFICATION
==================================================

Distinguish:

MINIMUM REQUIRED INFRASTRUCTURE PATH
from
EXTENDED MECHANISM-COVERAGE PATH

The minimum path should establish the infrastructure/protocol on the
least burdensome non-redundant configuration(s) needed before
expanding expensive verification.

The extended path may include additional non-redundant architectures
needed for the intended architecture-breadth claim.

Do not automatically require all surviving models to complete every
expensive adaptation scope.

In particular:

- conservative adapter paths may be verified before expert scopes;
- expert scopes are not automatically required;
- hybrid scopes should only be tested where they are part of the
  architecture-specific evidence we actually intend to claim.
