==================================================

- required entire completion cannot be retained
- frozen generation controls fundamentally unavailable
- actual model cannot satisfy required context/output contract

STOP configuration/model track.

- logging/instrumentation defect

QUARANTINE

→ invalidate affected/dependent gates

- telemetry missing
- determinism unresolved
- trace incomplete
- measurement cannot distinguish pass/fail

Action:
PENDING.

Create a machine-readable failure policy.
